HOW TO PLAY

You hold the bottom goal against 1–3 CPU pilots. Every goal you concede costs a point. Run out of points and your goal turns into a wall. The last pilot standing wins the round. Move while you hit to put spin on the ball.

DASH

Lunge forward and hit harder. Recharges in 2.2 seconds.

GRAB

Opens a 0.9-second catch window. Hold the ball for up to 1.2 seconds, then press X to throw. Where you caught it and how you're moving set the aim. Recharges in 4 seconds.

PICKUPS

  • SShield: blocks one goal (12s)
  • TSlows balls in your zone (6s)
  • MExtra ball (max 5)
  • WWide paddle (10s)

SETUP

Pick from 4 arenas and 4 difficulties, 1–3 CPUs, 5–25 starting points, and first to 1, 2, 3 or 5 round wins.

INSTALL

  1. Quickest: play it in your browser on the Lexaloffle BBS, or search for Orbit Rush in PICO-8's Splore.
  2. Get a licensed copy of PICO-8.
  3. Download orbit_rush.p8.png. Drag it onto PICO-8, then type run.
  4. Or run it from source: 
    git clone https://github.com/SpikeTheDragon40k/pico-orbit.git
cd pico-orbit
pico8 -run carts/orbit_rush.p8

The cart works on its own: you don't need a network connection or any extra files. It only reads controller zero.
