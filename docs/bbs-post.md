# Orbit Rush — PICO-8 Edition

Guard your goal. Bend the ball. Outlast the arena.

Orbit Rush is a single-player neon survival arcade game against one, two or three CPU rivals. Every missed ball costs a point. Eliminated goals become walls, changing the arena until one pilot remains.

- Four arenas: Neon Grid, Solar Core, Void Nebula and Ion Reactor.
- Four CPU difficulties and four cosmetic pilots.
- Moving-paddle spin, curved shots, DASH and GRAB/THROW.
- Shield, personal Slow-Mo, Multi-Ball and Wide pickups.
- Configurable starting points, round wins and timed extra balls.

**Controls:** Left/right moves your bottom paddle. O (Z) activates DASH. X catches or throws. Press X to arm a catch; after catching, move to aim and press X again to release, or wait for the automatic throw. Enter/P opens PICO-8's pause menu. Setup uses up/down to select and left/right to change options.

Original game, pilots and concept by SpikeTheDragon40k. A standalone PICO-8 demake of my original Orbit Rush.
